# Enterprise Retail AI Analytics — Executive Summary

## What this project demonstrates

A business-oriented retail analytics workflow covering:

- Sales and revenue analysis
- Profitability and margin analysis
- Customer 360 analysis
- Regional/category performance
- Returns and cancellations
- SQL business analysis
- Python exploratory analysis
- Excel dashboarding
- Power BI dashboarding
- Tableau dashboarding
- AI-driven risk interpretation

## Core business questions

1. Which regions and categories drive revenue and profit?
2. Where are margins strongest and weakest?
3. How are customer segments and retention changing?
4. Which operational issues reduce realized revenue?
5. Which products/customers deserve priority?
6. What risk signals should management investigate first?

## Final portfolio output

The repository is organized so a recruiter can start at `00_START_HERE`, understand the
business problem, open the dashboard artifacts, inspect SQL/Python, and review the
business insights and QA documentation.
