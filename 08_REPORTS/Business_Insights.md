# Business Insights

## 1. Revenue & profitability
The dashboards focus on revenue, gross profit and profit margin to distinguish growth
from profitable growth.

## 2. Customer performance
Customer 360 analysis highlights segment mix, unique-customer trend, top customers,
regional customer concentration and retention.

## 3. Operational performance
Order status, returns and cancellations are treated as operational KPIs because they
directly affect realized revenue and margin.

## 4. Regional performance
Regional revenue and customer counts help identify concentration and underperforming
markets.

## 5. Recommended business actions
- Investigate high-return categories and products.
- Reduce avoidable cancellations through order validation and customer communication.
- Protect high-value customer segments with retention programs.
- Prioritize high-margin categories and regions for growth investment.
- Monitor monthly trend changes rather than relying on a single-period KPI.
