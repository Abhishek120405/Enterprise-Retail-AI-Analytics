# 1. Project Overview

Enterprise Retail AI Analytics is a portfolio project designed to demonstrate the
full Data Analyst lifecycle: business problem definition, KPI design, SQL analysis,
Python EDA, dashboarding, customer analytics and AI-driven risk interpretation.

## Business problem

Retail businesses need a single analytical view of sales, customers, profitability
and operational leakage from returns/cancellations.

## Goal

Turn raw/analytical retail signals into decision-ready dashboards and business actions.
